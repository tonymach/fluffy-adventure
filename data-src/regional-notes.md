# Regional Notes (grows with every scan)

## EUROPE (verified July 2026)

### Visa layer, Canadian passport
- Schengen shared clock: 90 days in any 180 across ALL members combined. Members relevant to water scans: Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, Poland, Hungary, Slovenia, Croatia (joined 2023), Bulgaria and Romania (full members since Jan 1, 2025; the old "Bulgaria doesn't burn Schengen days" nomad trick is dead).
- Own-clock countries (each an independent 90 days, no Schengen burn): Albania, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Serbia, Bosnia.
- Georgia: 365 days visa-free for Canadians. The regional cheat code.
- Ukraine: 90 days on paper; active war, hard flag, not a viable base.
- Turkey: verify current e-visa/visa-free status for Canadians at run time.

### Long-stay unlocks (verify thresholds at run time, they drift)
- Estonia DNV: EUR 4,500/month GROSS, proven over prior 6 months (raised from 3,504). Fee ~EUR 100-120, up to 1 year, Type D. 183+ days in any 12 months = Estonian tax resident, 22% flat. VERIFIED July 2026.
- Croatia digital nomad residence: up to 12 months, foreign income not taxed locally.
- Latvia DNV, Hungary White Card, Montenegro digital nomad program: exist, check current numbers.

### Currency
- Bulgaria on the euro since Jan 1, 2026. Expect coastal prices to drift up over 2026-2027.
- Poland PLN, Hungary HUF, Serbia RSD, Albania ALL, Georgia GEL, North Macedonia MKD. Montenegro uses EUR unilaterally.

### Water temps, August
- Open Baltic: 17-21 C (wetsuit territory, race-realistic for northern events)
- Parnu Bay and Haapsalu's shallow bays: 20-23 C (warmest Baltic swimming)
- Lake Balaton: 24-26 C, plus the ~200 km Balatoni Bringakor bike loop and the annual 5.2 km cross-lake swim. Best swim-volume lake in the region.
- Adriatic: 24-27 C. Black Sea: 24-26 C, sandy entries on BG/RO coast.
- Lake Ohrid: 22-24 C at ~700 m altitude. Lake Bled: 22-24 C summer.

### Training geography
- Ironman Tallinn profile: flat bike, cool conditions. Baltic-based prep is race-specific (matching temps, flat quiet roads). Saaremaa and Hiiumaa are flat-road cycling heaven with near-zero traffic.
- Albanian riviera and Montenegro: spectacular climbing (Llogara, Lovcen) but sketchier drivers and tight shoulders.
- Masuria (PL) and Balaton (HU): rolling, quiet, lake-swim friendly.

## AFRICA (seeds, verify everything at scan time)
- Morocco: Essaouira, Taghazout, Mirleft. 90 days visa-free CAD. Atlantic is cold (18-21 C) but surf culture, cheap, 3h from Europe.
- Cape Verde: Sal, Mindelo. Easy entry (EASE fee). Wind sports capital.
- Egypt: Dahab (Red Sea, warm year-round, dirt cheap, world-class swimming). eVisa.
- Tanzania/Zanzibar: Paje, Nungwi. VOA/eVisa. Kenya coast: Diani. eTA.
- Mauritius: premium long-stay visa aimed at remote workers, free but income proof.
- South Africa: 90 days CAD. Cape Town, Wilderness, Jeffreys Bay. Load-shedding and safety layers needed in the scan.
- Senegal: 90 days CAD. Dakar peninsula, Popenguine.

## SOUTH AMERICA (seeds, verify everything at scan time)
- Canadian passport: ~90 days visa-free almost everywhere. Exceptions: Brazil requires an eVisa (reinstated April 2025), Bolivia requires a visa.
- Standouts to seed the inventory: Brazil (Jericoacoara, Pipa, Florianopolis), Uruguay (Punta del Este, La Paloma), Peru (Mancora), Ecuador (Ayampe, Olon, Montanita), Colombia (Santa Marta, Palomino; check security layer), Chile lake district (Pucon, Puerto Varas), Argentina lakes (Bariloche, Villa La Angostura).
- Seasons invert: their summer is Dec-Mar. Peak pricing flips accordingly.

## SOUTHEAST ASIA (seeds)

## SCAN UPDATES, verified July 2 2026 (appended after Eastern Europe + Africa run)

### Europe corrections (supersede cached notes above)
- Albania: the 365-day visa-free allowance is US-passport only. Canadians get standard 90/180 (still own-clock, no Schengen burn). All Albanian riviera towns (Saranda, Vlore, Dhermi, Himare, Durres) flagged for dangerous coastal-road cycling; Durres also water-quality near port.
- Croatia DNV income floor now EUR 3,622.50/month (up from ~3,295), auto-indexed annually. Residence up to 18 months. Foreign income untaxed locally. Health insurance min applies.
- Estonia DNV health-insurance minimum: EUR 30,000 coverage. Latvia DNV ~EUR 3,400/mo, insurance min ~EUR 42,600.
- Georgia now mandates travel insurance (30,000 GEL min) since Jan 2026, still 365 days visa-free.
- Montenegro DNV sunsets Dec 31, 2026 with no confirmed extension; income EUR 1,800-2,400/mo. Apply by ~Oct to clear processing.
- EES (Schengen Entry/Exit System) went live Oct 12, 2025. 90/180 is now biometrically enforced; own-clock stacking should avoid any "de facto residence" pattern.
- Ironman Tallinn confirmed Aug 22, 2026 (flat sub-4% bike).

### Africa corrections (supersede seeds above)
- Namibia flipped to visa-required Apr 2025 (~USD 90). Swakopmund still scores on merit but no longer visa-free. Much of the web is stale on this.
- Malawi flipped to visa-required Jan 2026 (USD 75). Lake Malawi (Cape Maclear, Nkhata Bay) swimmable and warm but bilharzia risk real (praziquantel post-trip), limited medical, malaria.
- Kenya now uses eTA (USD ~30), grants 90 days. Diani: fly into Ukunda, avoid Likoni Ferry.
- Zanzibar adds mandatory USD 44 inbound insurance. Paje swim is tide-locked (train at high tide).
- Egypt: eVisa/VOA 30 days, extendable. Dahab has a free Sinai-Only 15-day entry (no visa) if you stay in the Sinai peninsula. El Gouna = best Egyptian internet; Dahab internet is weak (founder catch).
- Mauritius Premium Visa: 1 year renewable, income proof ~EUR 1,500/month. Best all-round safety tier on the African board.
- Cape Verde: 30-day EASE exemption (airport fee, not a visa) OR Remote Working visa 6 months renewable to 12, ~EUR 1,500/mo, exempts local tax residency.
- South Africa load-shedding effectively over (400+ clean days as of mid-2026). Garden Route (Wilderness, Sedgefield) underrated: freshwater lagoon open-water swim + elite bike/run, George airport close, good fiber. Weak rand = best continental value now.
- Mozambique north (Cabo Delgado) Avoid-All-Travel; Tofo and Vilanculos sit far south and safe, still flagged. Mozambique ETA visa-exempt 30 days.
- Tunisia ambiguity: travel.gc.ca says 90 days, IATA/Timatic grants Canadians 4 months. Confirm with consulate before relying on it.
- Saly (Senegal) is a true package-tourism enclave (Anti-Cancun fail). Stone Town (Zanzibar) weak as a training base (working port, no swim).
